Clouds aren’t a backdrop

They are immense objects
scattered through space.

Your eyes are only centimetres apart. At the scale of a landscape, almost everything appears flat. But take two photographs hundreds of metres apart and depth suddenly returns.

Move through the scene

Scroll turns distance
into separation.

As your viewpoint travels, nearby forms shift faster than distant ones. That parallax is the raw material of stereoscopic depth.

Captured from a plane

How to see it

Let a third picture appear.

1

Look between the two frames and gently cross your eyes.

2

Let the images drift until matching details overlap.

3

Hold the middle image softly. The scene will open into depth.

If it feels strained, stop and relax your eyes. A smaller image is often easier at first.
